✦ CHAPTER V ✦
✨ Authored Among The Constellations ✨

Written In Starlight

“If every star were a memory, the cosmos still couldn't hold all my love for you.”

I remember when you told me a few days ago, "I don't think you miss me."

So let me show you how you live in every thought, every silence, and every heartbeat of mine...

I think of you in quiet rooms, when shadows start to grow,

In idle hours staring up, where silent thoughts flow.

And even when the room is full, amidst the bustling crowd,

I look down at my glowing screen and wish for you aloud.

I think of you on quiet roofs beneath the midnight air,

Tracing stars and silver moons, imagining you there.

The sky expands in infinite blue, endless, deep, and grand,

Yet feels so small compared to how I long to hold your hand.

I think of you through weary days, when heavy hours fall,

When dark clouds gather round my heart, your memory clears it all.

I lie awake in quiet night with just one tender plea—

To close my eyes and wake up with you resting next to me.

Through silent, drifting nebulae and cosmic seas so deep,

You are the softest, sweetest dream my soul will forever keep.

No distance in this universe could ever dim your light,

You are my dawn in darkness and my star through every night.

“You are, and will always be, my entire universe.” ✨🌌

Yours, until the last star burns out...

Forever Authored By, Chhavi
