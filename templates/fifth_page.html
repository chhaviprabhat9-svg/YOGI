✨ Authored Among The Constellations ✨

Written In Starlight

“If every star was a memory, the cosmos still couldn't hold all my love for you.”

In a universe of infinite space and endless time,

My favorite miracle will always be that you are mine.

Out of billions of stars floating softly through the night,

Your smile is the only light that makes my world bright.

Through silent nebulae and quiet cosmos so deep,

You are the sweetest dream that I will forever keep.

“You are, and will always be, my entire universe.” ✨🌌

Yours, until the last star burns out...

Forever Authored By, Chhavi
